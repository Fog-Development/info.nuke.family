# Torn Resources Guide

A curated list of tools, userscripts, websites, and guides that the Nuke Family has found useful in Torn. Most items here are community-built and are not officially endorsed by us - use them at your own discretion and review what you install.

:::warning[A note on userscripts]
Userscripts run with significant permissions in your browser. Only install scripts from sources you trust, and prefer linking through Greasy Fork or the author's official GitHub repository (which is what every link below does) rather than re-hosted copies.
:::

## Nuke Family Tools

### Nuke Assistant

Our own faction helper script. Lets revivers see who is in an active contract directly from a Torn profile page, lets all members view and contribute to the blacklist, and ties into your [nuke.family](https://nuke.family/user) account. Requires a Nuke Family site API key.

**Author:** Fogest [2254826]

**Link:** [Nuke Blacklist Userscript guide](../userscripts/nuke_assistant_userscript.md)

---

## Mobile & All-in-One Apps

### Torn PDA

An all-in-one mobile assistant for Torn, officially endorsed by Torn's developers and used by 40,000+ players. Offers stat tracking, chain management, revive calling, travel notifications, and many other quality-of-life shortcuts so you don't have to swap between apps mid-action.

**Link:** [Torn PDA Forum Thread](https://www.torn.com/forums.php#/p=threads&f=67&t=16163503&b=0&a=0)

## Websites & Web Tools

### Torn Report

A growing collection of luck/performance reports - Trick or Treat, Looting, Mugging, City Finds, Organized Crimes, Supply Packs, Crimes, Dump Search, Energy, Poker, Armor Cache, Black Friday, Christmas Town, Slots and more. Great for seeing if RNG is actually screwing you or if you just feel that way.

**Link:** [torn.report](https://torn.report/)

---

### YATA (Yet Another Torn App)

A long-standing companion site built on the official Torn API. Includes a bazaar tracker, company information, extensive faction data, target list management, merit/award progress tracking, and looting information.

**Link:** [yata.yt](https://yata.yt/)

---

### Torn Stats

A great place to analyze your stat growth over time, chart your gains, and compare yourself to others in your faction in an easy-to-read view.

**Link:** [tornstats.com](https://www.tornstats.com/)

---

### GongTools

Built by a current Nuke Family member. Includes a faction perks upgrade simulator (useful for faction leaders), a gym training optimizer, a stakeout tool, and a market scan tool.

**Link:** [gongtools.dev](https://gongtools.dev/)

---

### Torn Travel Planner

Plans efficient trading routes across the Torn travel destinations to maximize profit per trip.

**Link:** [torntravel.com](https://www.torntravel.com/)

Predicts total battle stats for each Torn player almost everywhere they appear (profiles, faction pages, attack lists, hall of fame, and more) using a machine-learning model trained on real spies. Predictions can also be overridden by linking with TornStats/YATA so you see the most reliable info available.

**Author:** TDup

**Link:** [GitHub](https://github.com/tdup-torn/userscripts)

## Crimes (OCs & Crimes 2.0)

### Lazy Crimes

Adds a "spam crime" button at the top of the Crimes page that redirects to whatever the next sensible button is. Built for players who finished all merits and have every crime maxed and just want fewer clicks.

**Author:** Heartflower \[2626587\]

**Link:** [Greasy Fork](https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/540604-lazy-crimes)

---

### Crime Profitability

Displays value-per-nerve on every crime page, sourced from the community Crime Profitability Index spreadsheet by Emforus. Data is fetched once every 24 hours and cached locally - no API key required.

**Author:** Heartflower \[2626587\]

**Link:** [Greasy Fork](https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/538188-crime-profitability)
