## Looking for the Nuke Assistant?

The Nuke Assistant is our own faction helper script. It lets revivers see who is in an active contract directly from a Torn profile page, lets all members view and contribute to the blacklist, and ties into your [nuke.family](https://nuke.family/user) account.

**Installation & usage guide:** [Nuke Blacklist Userscript](./nuke_assistant_userscript.md)
